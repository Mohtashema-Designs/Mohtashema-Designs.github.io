About Mohtashema

Founded by Reham Adel, Mohtashema Designs is an Egyptian modest luxury fashion brand that blends timeless modesty with modern elegance. Every piece—from flowing kaftans and breathable linen sets to tailored wool coats—is thoughtfully crafted using premium fabrics and flattering silhouettes. Proudly offering custom sizing and delivery across Egypt, we design refined, effortless fashion made to fit your unique style.
